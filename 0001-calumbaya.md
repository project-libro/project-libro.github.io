# Notes
Over the last few years, I’ve given my old books to places like the local library. But this time, I’m donating them to a local elementary school so kids who really need them can read them! I also bought a bunch of extra bags of books at the library book sale for only $5 a bag just to add to the donation.

<img alt="Book sale" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/75bf568d-1fc3-4d93-8398-b91f5e1946a9"  style="width: 100%; height: auto; max-width: 2048px;" />

When I first decided to launch a book drive to support an elementary school in the Philippines, I envisioned a smooth, rewarding process. But along the  way I had my challenges that I had to face and helped me learn the power of determination and community.

Some challenges I had at the moment were collecting and transporting my books. I had to wait when there was a library donation event or at school. Even after successfully gathering the books, a new obstacle arose: the costs of shipping heavy boxes internationally.

<img alt="Packing to ship" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/87b5a578-7310-417e-8e89-40ea2653e2ab"  style="width: 100%; height: auto; max-width: 2048px;" />


As my family and I traveled through the Philippines, the most meaningful part of our trip was visiting Calumbaya Elementary School. There, I was able to donate more than 500 books that I had worked hard to collect. When I arrived, I went to the principal’s office, where Dr. Somera, the teachers, and the staff welcomed me warmly and excitedly.

<img alt="Meeting with the local teachers" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/852d31f7-a600-4ce6-9951-c8282bb2253f"  style="width: 100%; height: auto; max-width: 2048px;" />

Soon after, we opened the heavy boxes, and the teachers brought the students over to see the books. The room quickly filled with excitement as the children gathered around with wide eyes. Some students were so eager that they picked up books and ran outside to read together on the benches.


<img alt="Kids in the classroom" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/528d6024-7923-49de-8b64-46e2cd013b9c"  style="width: 100%; height: auto; max-width: 2048px;" />

Watching them share the books and enjoy reading made me feel proud of what I had done. Seeing their happiness filled my heart, and I will never forget how something as simple as a book brought so much joy to their school community.


<img alt="Happy kids" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b24ec1a4-6f39-4ba0-9b91-31075ed00ae6"  style="width: 100%; height: auto; max-width: 2048px;" />

Seeing the happiness on the children's faces filled my heart in a way I had never experienced before. Watching them read gave me a sense of pride, making all the stressful weeks of planning and heavy lifting into a distant memory. This project taught me that no obstacle is impossible when you are working toward a greater good. Knowing that something as simple as a book could bring so much joy and opportunity to a community is a lesson in gratitude and perseverance that I will carry with me for the rest of my life.
