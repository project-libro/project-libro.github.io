# Notes
Over the last few years, I’ve given my old books to places like the local library. But this time, I’m donating them to a local elementary school so kids who really need them can read them! I also bought a bunch of extra bags of books at the library book sale for only $5 a bag just to add to the donation.

<img width="829" height="718" alt="Book sale" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/75bf568d-1fc3-4d93-8398-b91f5e1946a9" />

When I first decided to launch a book drive to support an elementary school in the Philippines, I envisioned a smooth, rewarding process. But along the  way I had my challenges that I had to face and helped me learn the power of determination and community.

Some challenges I had at the moment were collecting and transporting my books. I had to wait when there was a library donation event or at school. Even after successfully gathering the books, a new obstacle arose: the costs of shipping heavy boxes internationally.

<img width="854" height="812" alt="Packing to ship" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/87b5a578-7310-417e-8e89-40ea2653e2ab" />

As we traveled through the Philippines, the most meaningful stop of our journey was Calumbaya Elementary School, where I was finally able to deliver over 500 hard-earned books. Stepping into the principal’s office, Dr. Somera and the school staff welcomed me with overwhelming glee and warmth.

<img width="2048" height="1536" alt="497b493445251a225b7aa410af4ff697" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/852d31f7-a600-4ce6-9951-c8282bb2253f" />

Soon after, we opened the heavy box, and the teachers brought the students over to see the surprise. The atmosphere instantly shifted into pure electricity. The children gathered around the boxes with wide eyes and palpable excitement. Some of them literally couldn't wait; they grabbed books and ran straight outside to start reading together on the school benches. Sharing the joy of these books with one another was a beautiful sight to behold.

<img width="2048" height="922" alt="b24825427ce0746ca27e2a46704e4985" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/528d6024-7923-49de-8b64-46e2cd013b9c" />

<img width="2048" height="922" alt="b9f419e8b6b64791a3c228c239c27895" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b24ec1a4-6f39-4ba0-9b91-31075ed00ae6" />

Seeing the happiness on the children's faces filled my heart in a way I had never experienced before. Watching them read gave me a sense of pride, making all the stressful weeks of planning and heavy lifting into a distant memory. This project taught me that no obstacle is impossible when you are working toward a greater good. Knowing that something as simple as a book could bring so much joy and opportunity to a community is a lesson in gratitude and perseverance that I will carry with me for the rest of my life.
